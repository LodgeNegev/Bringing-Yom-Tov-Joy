Yahrzeit Reminder Project

Remembering those who came before us

Lodge Negev provides a complimentary Yahrzeit reminder service to help families remember the Hebrew anniversary of the passing of a loved one.

There is no charge for the service.

Those who wish to make a donation in memory of a loved one are invited to support Lodge Negev's charitable activities.
May their memory be for a blessing.
